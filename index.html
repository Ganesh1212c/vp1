Built to waste less

Our sustainability work

Reduce

Efficient cutting patterns on our box templates and renewable power cut waste and energy use. Our goal is 75% less paper waste.

Reuse

Production waste goes back to paper mills, and we ask customers to return used shippers.

Recycle

We recycle paper and replace lamination with water-based aqueous and UV coatings.

It started with one shoe box

In 1997, Mr. V J Cherian started Vettiyil with a 500 sq ft workshop and a single product: shoe boxes for local footwear brands. His son, Mr. George Cherian, joined him to widen the range for healthcare, food, electronics and other industries.

Today the company runs three plants and more than 80 machines. It still treats every order the way it treated that first box.
