From a 500 sq ft workshop to three plants.

Vettiyil Packaging Pvt Ltd makes corrugated boxes, printed boxes and grey board boxes. Premium raw materials and modern production technology mean every order meets rigorous industrial standards.

We keep up with industry trends so we can offer packaging made for each client's needs, and our team works closely with customers on customised options and support after delivery.

Our history

Five steps in how a shoe-box workshop became a packaging manufacturer.

  1. 1997

    A humble start

    Vettiyil began in 500 sq ft of space, driven by passion and a commitment to deliver quality. It has since grown into a trusted name in packaging.

    500 sq ft
  2. 2001

    Scaling up shoe box production

    Demand grew, so we expanded from 500 to 2,000 sq ft. More capacity meant faster delivery of quality packaging and a stronger place in the market.

    2,000 sq ft
  3. 2013

    A leap in manufacturing

    We moved into a 25,000 sq ft facility. It let us take on larger orders, widen our range and deliver premium packaging at scale.

    25,000 sq ft
  4. 2018

    Printing brought in-house

    A new in-house printing machine and a 40,000 sq ft facility gave us more room for customisation, precision and efficiency.

    40,000 sq ft
  5. 2024

    A third plant in Coimbatore

    Our third facility, in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, serves customers in Tamil Nadu and Kerala more effectively.

    Up to 65,000 sq ft

Our commitment to quality

In packaging, steady production, strict quality control and low waste matter most. Our checks begin at the raw material and design stage and continue through every step of manufacturing. Every finished order is inspected before it leaves.

Our team trains regularly on the testing equipment, which includes:

  • GSM and caliper testing machines
  • pH and moisture content testers
  • Bursting strength and burst factor analysers
  • Edge crush strength testers
  • Box compression strength machines

A test report with every dispatch

Every order from Vettiyil arrives with a full test report, attached to your invoice copy. It works as a certificate of the quality of your cartons.
